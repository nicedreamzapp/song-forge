# Privacy Policy — Song Forge (Android & iOS)

**Effective date: July 14, 2026**

Song Forge is built by Nice Dreamz LLC. This policy covers the Song Forge mobile app (also known as OwnATune) on Android and iOS.

## The short version

Song Forge makes songs from a description you type. To do that, the words you type are sent to our own servers (in Humboldt County, California) so we can create your song. **We do not require an account, we do not ask for your name or email, and we do not sell or share your data with anyone.**

## What is sent to us, and why

- **Your song description / prompt.** When you tap "Make my song," the text you typed is sent to our servers so the song can be generated. This is the only way the app can work.
- **A random app ID.** The first time you open the app, it creates a random identifier and stores it on your device. It is sent with your requests only so we can keep track of how many free songs you have left and any songs you've purchased. It is not your name, phone number, advertising ID, or any real-world identity — just a random string tied to this install.

That's it. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or any device identifiers beyond the random app ID above.

## Your finished songs

Finished songs are delivered to your device and are 100% yours, with full commercial rights. We do not keep copies of your finished songs on our servers.

## What we do NOT do

- No accounts, no sign-in, no passwords.
- No advertising, no ad SDKs, no trackers, no third-party analytics.
- We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.
- No big-tech AI clouds — songs are generated on our own hardware.

## Purchases

If you buy a song pack, the purchase is processed by Google Play (Android) or Apple (iOS). We receive only a confirmation that the purchase was valid so we can add songs to your balance. We never see your card details.

## Children

Song Forge is intended for general audiences (13+) and is not directed at children.

## Data deletion

Because there are no accounts, we hold no personal profile to delete. Deleting the app removes the random app ID and your local song box from your device.
